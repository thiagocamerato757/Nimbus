# First Selection is recorded, not derived or enforced

Before each Game's draft the analyst records which Team held First Selection, what it chose, and the resulting Side and Draft Position of both Teams; only the result is required. How the right is earned is not publicly documented, may vary by league or change mid-season, and blocking the analyst during a live Series is worse than trusting them. A Title profile may configure a default holder to pre-fill (for example the loser of the previous Game), which the analyst can always override.

## Considered Options

- The engine derives and enforces the holder from a rule in the profile or a policy chosen per Series: rejected for the reasons above.
