# Coach flags steer the code shortlist; notes go only to the LLM

A coach knows things the data does not, and saving Characters for later Games is the central Fearless decision, so it must work without an LLM.

- **Flags** mark a Character as Priority or Avoid (Team or one of our Players), Ban target (against one opponent) or Save until Game N (Team or our Player: bottom band with "saved for Game N" before it, Priority from Game N on). A flag applies to one Series or the Workspace.
- Code applies flags: they move a Character to the high or bottom band, show why, never hide it, and never make an illegal Character eligible.
- A Workspace flag may expire on a date or "until patch X"; expired flags stop applying and are listed for review.
- **Notes** are short free text per Series and per opponent Team, given only to the LLM, never overriding legality. Reasoning that comes from a note is shown apart from data reasons ("per your note: ..."), and figures in notes may be referenced but never restated as Statistics.
- The Suggestion Log records the flags and notes in effect.

## Considered Options

- Data only: the coach would apply their knowledge by hand under time pressure.
- Notes only: nothing would work without an LLM, and the model could hand the coach's opinion back as analysis.
