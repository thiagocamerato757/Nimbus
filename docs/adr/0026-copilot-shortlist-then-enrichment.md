# The copilot shows a code shortlist at once and enriches it with the LLM

A pick turn lasts seconds and an LLM answer takes several, and the board must never show an empty copilot on a live turn.

- On every state change, code immediately produces a ranked shortlist from computed data, without prose. The LLM's reranking and reasons replace it when they arrive. With no LLM, the copilot degrades to the shortlist instead of disappearing.
- **Eligibility:** picks are legal Characters that someone in the advised Lineup without a pick yet has Available Now; bans are legal Characters the opposing Lineup can play. With no pool data for the relevant Players, all legal Characters ranked by presence, with a visible notice. Player Assignments come after the draft, so who holds each earlier pick is inferred as in [ADR 0023](0023-pick-role-resolution.md) (Lineup pool match, then Observed Role Usage); when uncertain, eligibility widens, so a wrong guess never hides a valid option.
- **Ranking:** ordered factors, not a weighted score, since there is nothing yet to tune weights with. The first factor is grouped into high, medium and low bands (thresholds are profile settings) and later factors order within a band, so a one-Game difference cannot override everything else. Bans: opponent presence and pick priority, then how few comparable options the likely Player has left, then recency. Picks: how contested the Character is (opponent pool and priority), banded; then within a band whether it is in a Declared Pool, then the advised Team's pick rate, then win rate. Pick order should follow contest, not only comfort: a contested Character must be taken before the opponent can, while an uncontested comfort pick can wait, and a band then means the same thing for bans, picks and Flags. Each entry states why it sits there: a declared Character in a low band for lack of contest says so ("uncontested: can wait for a later turn"), and one eligible only through an Observed Pool says so with its statistic ("played 4 times; not in the declared pool"). The LLM may choose any eligible Character.
- The LLM (the prepared-suggestions Model Role, [ADR 0030](0030-llm-providers-behind-one-interface.md); manual refresh uses a stronger one) starts ahead, as soon as the previous action is entered; the analyst can refresh manually or switch this off. When the state changes, the in-flight request is cancelled, and a stale answer is never shown as current.
- Up to three ranked options, each eligible as above, with one or two expandable lines of reason, the Lineup Players who can play it, their Role and any low-confidence flag. Where the advised Team has consecutive turns of one type, options are combinations covering them together.
- Reasons follow [ADR 0021](0021-numbers-are-code-interpretation-is-ai.md): figures only from statistic ids, and general knowledge labelled.
- Advice only: nothing reaches the board unless the analyst records what happened. The advised Team is configurable (Our Team by default), with an on-request "what might they do?" for the other.
- Every suggestion is logged with the state it was for, what was actually recorded on that turn, latency and tokens used, to measure follow rate and the cost of preparing ahead.
